Entry for the make.fun bounty **news-image-20261118 — "Sum up 18 November's world news in one image"**.

## The image

A newspaper front page dated **WORLD NEWS — NOVEMBER 18, 2026**, in four
panels:

1. **Top left — the Gulf:** missile streaks and explosions over a moonlit
   strait, warships below — the US strike on Qeshm Island and Iran's
   retaliation against Bahrain and Kuwait.
2. **Top right — the Kerch Bridge:** the bridge to Crimea burning and
   partially collapsing into the sea under a Ukrainian drone strike.
3. **Bottom left — Shenzhen:** three leaders shaking hands at the APEC
   summit, Chinese and Russian flags behind them, ahead of the
   Trump–Xi–Putin trilateral meeting.
4. **Bottom right — Washington and health:** the US Senate chamber with a
   ballot box and a rising blue wave of seats — Democrat Mary Peltola's
   Alaska win giving Democrats 56 seats — plus a doctor with a vaccine
   vial, for the day's health news: the US losing its measles-free status
   and the Ebola outbreak in DR Congo.

## Run it

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts
```

This copies `entry.png` to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.png` (or
`/output/entry.png` when the variable is not set). The program uses only the
Node.js standard library, makes no network requests, and always produces the
same output bytes.
